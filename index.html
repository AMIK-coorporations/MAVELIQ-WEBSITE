Beauty shopping guide

Shopping for Beauty and Personal Care Online in Pakistan

More and more people in Pakistan now buy their makeup, skincare and fragrance online. Shopping from home makes it easy to compare prices in rupees, read what a product is for and check whether it is in stock — without visiting several shops in person. Maveliq is built for exactly that: one place to explore lipsticks, face washes, serums, perfumes, makeup essentials, hair colour and everyday personal-care products.

Choosing products that suit you

Every skin type, tone and routine is different, so the best product is the one that matches your needs rather than the most talked-about one. Start with the basics — a cleanser that suits your skin, a moisturiser and daily sun protection — and add serums, masks or scrubs one at a time. Read each listing's description and directions, check the pack for ingredients, and patch-test anything new before using it on your face.

Makeup, fragrance and colour

For makeup, choose foundation and powder shades in natural daylight and match them to your jawline. Matte lipsticks give a soft, velvety finish, while lip liners help colour stay neat for longer. With perfumes and body mists, remember that the same scent can last differently on different skin and in different weather, so apply to moisturised skin and pulse points for the best result.
