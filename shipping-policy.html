Shipping Policy

Delivery across Pakistan — charges, timelines and tracking explained clearly.

Live figures at checkout

The exact delivery charge for your order — and whether it qualifies for free delivery — is always shown on the cart and checkout pages before you confirm.

Delivery areas

We deliver nationwide across Pakistan. Please make sure your delivery address and phone number are complete and correct, including house or flat number, street, area and a nearby landmark where possible.

Delivery charges

  • A standard delivery charge applies to orders below the free-delivery threshold.
  • Orders at or above the free-delivery threshold ship free.
  • The current charge and threshold are shown in your cart and at checkout before you place the order.

Timelines

  • Processing. Orders are reviewed and confirmed before dispatch. We contact you on the phone number provided if we need to verify anything.
  • Dispatch. Confirmed orders are handed to the courier promptly on working days.
  • Transit. Estimated delivery timelines are shown at checkout and in your confirmation. These are estimates; occasional delays can happen with courier networks.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next working day.

Order tracking

Every order gets a reference number. Use the Track Your Order page with your reference and the phone number used at checkout to see the current status. Once your parcel is dispatched, any courier tracking number is shown there as well.

Failed delivery attempts

If the courier cannot reach you, they will attempt to contact the phone number provided. Please respond so the parcel can be redelivered. Repeated failed attempts may result in the order being returned to us — in that case we'll contact you to arrange the next step.

Checking your parcel

Please inspect the parcel when it arrives. If the package is visibly damaged, you may refuse to accept it, or note the issue with the courier at handover. Report any problem through our Contact page with your order reference as soon as possible.

Cash on Delivery

Where Cash on Delivery is offered, please keep the exact amount ready where you can — it helps the courier complete the delivery smoothly.

Questions

Anything unclear about delivery? Get in touch — we're happy to help.
