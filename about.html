Beauty, Care & Everyday Essentials.

Our Story

Maveliq is an online store focused on beauty, skincare, cosmetics, and personal-care products. Our goal is to make exploring these products more convenient by bringing product information, categories, and ordering options together in one accessible place.

We want customers to understand what they are browsing, compare the available options, and review important information before making a purchase.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a convenient and transparent online shopping experience for customers looking for beauty and personal-care essentials.

We aim to provide clear product information, straightforward ordering, and helpful customer support throughout the shopping journey.

How we work

Our Approach

Clear Information

We aim to provide accurate descriptions, prices, available options, and usage information for listed products.

Convenient Shopping

Our website is designed to help customers browse collections and place orders using their preferred supported device.

Customer Communication

We want customers to have access to clear ordering information and a way to contact us when they need assistance.

Responsible Product Presentation

We aim to present products honestly, without unsupported claims or misleading information.

Our Partnership with DIEFEI 3D

Maveliq partners with DIEFEI 3D to offer selected DIEFEI 3D products. Customers can explore the products available through this partnership in our dedicated collection.

Our Commitment

We aim to keep our product information, prices, order details, and published policies clear and accurate. As our catalogue develops, we will continue working to make the shopping experience easier to navigate and understand.
