About Maveliq

A straightforward online store for beauty and personal care — built for customers across Pakistan.

Who we are

Maveliq is an independent, online-first beauty and personal-care store. We bring together everyday essentials — cleansers, moisturisers, serums, lip makeup, fragrance, body care and hair care — in one place, with clear prices in Rupees and honest availability shown on every product page.

Our goal is simple: make it easy to find products you actually need, know what you'll pay, and have them delivered to your door anywhere in Pakistan.

How we work

  • Clear pricing. Every price is shown in PKR, with original prices displayed only where a genuine discount applies.
  • Honest stock. Availability and low-stock warnings are shown live, so you know what's on hand before you order.
  • Tracked delivery. Orders are packed with care and dispatched with a reference number you can track any time.
  • Real support. A real person answers your questions — reach us through any contact channel listed on our Contact page.

Our promise

We stand behind the products we list and describe them accurately. Where a product has limited public information, we say so rather than guess. If something isn't right with your order, our returns policy explains exactly what happens next.
