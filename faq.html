Product Care & Use

The DIEFEI 3D products we list are offered through our partnership with DIEFEI 3D. Each listing shows the product's name, options and price, and you can always check the packaging on delivery. If anything about an item looks wrong, contact us straight away with your order reference.

Not every skincare or cosmetic product suits everyone. Read the description, ingredients and warnings, and do a patch test on a small area — for example the inner arm — 24 hours before using a new product on your face. If you have a skin condition or any concern, ask a qualified healthcare professional first.

Follow the storage directions on the pack. As a general rule, keep products closed, in a cool and dry place, away from direct sunlight and heat — not on a sunny windowsill or in a hot car — and use clean hands or applicators to avoid contamination.

Check the packaging for the manufacturing or expiry date and any batch details. Many products also show a small open-jar symbol (for example 12M) that tells you how many months to use them after opening. If a date is unclear, contact us before using the product.
