Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ordering, payment, delivery and returns. Can't find what you need? Contact us.

Ordering

Browse the catalogue, choose your shade or size where applicable, and add items to your cart. Open the cart to review quantities and totals, then choose Proceed to Checkout. Enter your delivery details, pick a payment method and place the order. You'll see an order reference on screen — please keep it.

No. You can check out as a guest with just your name, phone number and delivery address. We only ask for what we need to deliver your order.

Please contact us as soon as possible with your order reference. Changes or cancellations are possible while the order is still being processed; once it has been dispatched, our returns policy applies.

Payment

Cash on Delivery is offered where it is enabled. If Cash on Delivery appears as a selectable option at checkout, you can pay in cash when your order arrives. Other payment methods are shown as “coming soon” until they are properly connected and verified.

Yes — every price on the site is shown in PKR. Any discount shown compares the current price with the product's regular price; we only display genuine reductions.

Orders are validated on our server before they are accepted — prices, stock and totals are always re-checked, so what you see at confirmation is what you pay. We never store payment card details on this website.

Delivery

We deliver nationwide across Pakistan. The delivery charge and any free-delivery threshold are shown on the cart and checkout pages before you confirm.

Estimated delivery timelines are shown at checkout and in your order confirmation. Orders are dispatched after confirmation, and you'll be contacted on your phone if we need more details.

Open Track Your Order and enter your order reference together with the phone number used at checkout. Both are required, which keeps your details private. Once dispatched, any courier tracking number is shown there too.

Returns & products

Eligible items can be returned according to our Returns & Refund Policy. Contact us with your order reference and a short description (and a photo where relevant) to start the process.

We source our catalogue carefully and describe each product accurately on its page. Where full details (such as ingredients or manufacturer information) aren't published, we say so rather than guess — you're always welcome to ask before buying.

Out-of-stock items can't be ordered. Please contact us and we'll let you know when it's expected back, or suggest a similar alternative from the catalogue.
