1. Who we are

Maveliq (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an online beauty and personal-care store operating in Pakistan. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Data controller contact: [owner to add business name, address and email for privacy enquiries].

2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to process and deliver your order:

  • Order details — full name, phone number, delivery address and city.
  • Optional details — email address and delivery instructions, if you choose to provide them.
  • Order history — the products you ordered, quantities, amounts and status, linked to your order reference.
  • Basic technical data — such as device and browser information that may be logged automatically by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

We do not collect payment card numbers on this website. Cash on Delivery orders involve no online payment data at all.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, fulfil and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your order (for example, to confirm delivery details).
  • To handle returns, refunds and support enquiries.
  • To keep basic business records as required by law.
  • To protect the website against fraud and misuse.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Who we share it with

To deliver your order we share the minimum necessary details (name, phone number, address) with:

  • Courier partners who deliver your parcel.
  • Service providers that host this website and its database, under agreements that limit their use of the data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order information for as long as needed to deliver your order, handle any returns or disputes, and meet record-keeping obligations. When it is no longer required, it is deleted or anonymised. [Owner to confirm the retention period.]

6. Cookies and local storage

This website stores small amounts of data in your browser to keep your shopping cart working between pages and to remember basic preferences. These are essential for the site to function. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings, though your cart contents will be lost.

If analytics or advertising cookies are added later, this policy and the cookie notice will be updated and consent will be requested where required.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to legal record-keeping duties.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details on our Contact page. We may need to verify your identity first.

8. Keeping your information safe

We use access controls, authentication and database security rules so that customer information can only be read by authorised people and legitimate order processes. Sensitive operations are performed on secure servers rather than in your browser.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we review our protections regularly.

9. Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The “last updated” date at the top will change when we do, and significant changes may be highlighted on the site.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us through the channels on our Contact page.
