Returns & Refunds

If something isn't right with your order, here's exactly how we put it right.

Our return commitment

We want you to be happy with your order. If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect item, contact us and we'll arrange a replacement, exchange or refund in line with this policy.

Return window

Returns must be requested within [owner to confirm, e.g. 7 days] of delivery. Contact us with your order reference as soon as you notice an issue.

What can be returned

Eligible (typically):

  • Items that arrived damaged or broken.
  • Items that are defective or faulty.
  • Wrong items delivered, or items that don't match your order.
  • Sealed, unopened and unused items, if you change your mind — subject to the conditions below.

What can't be returned

  • Products that have been opened, used or unsealed — for hygiene reasons, cosmetics, skincare and personal-care items cannot be returned once the seal is broken.
  • Items damaged after delivery through misuse or improper storage.
  • Products past their expiry date at the time of the claim.
  • Items without proof of purchase (your order reference is enough).

How to start a return

  1. Contact us through the channels on our Contact page.
  2. Provide your order reference, the item name and a short description of the issue. A photo helps us resolve things faster.
  3. Wait for our confirmation and instructions before sending anything back — we'll arrange the pickup or return address where applicable.
  4. Pack the item securely, ideally in its original packaging.

Refunds

  • Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we'll approve or reject the refund and let you know.
  • Cash on Delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or another agreed method, since no online payment was taken.
  • Refund processing times depend on your bank or payment provider once we initiate the transfer.
  • Where a replacement is preferred, we'll send it as soon as the return is confirmed.

Return shipping

For our error (wrong, damaged or defective item), we cover the return cost. For change-of-mind returns, the return delivery cost may be borne by the customer. [Owner to confirm and adjust.]

Exchanges

Want a different shade or size? Subject to availability, we're happy to exchange — the same eligibility rules apply. If the new item costs more, we'll let you know the difference before proceeding.

Need help?

Not sure whether your order qualifies? Just ask — we'd rather clarify than have you guessing. Contact our team.
